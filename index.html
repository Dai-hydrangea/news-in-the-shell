WELCOME ─ NEWS IN THE SHELL

RSS keyword orrery, browser-only, BYOF (bring your own feeds)

This tool is a visualization shell for RSS feeds you provide.
It does not bundle any news content — you add the RSS feeds, you choose what to watch.

All article copyrights belong to their original publishers. Tap OPEN ARTICLE → to read on the original site.

Because of browser CORS, you'll need a small proxy. The default points to a public CORS proxy (rate-limited). For stable use, set up your own (see docs).
